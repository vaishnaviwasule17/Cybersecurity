# Cybersecurity Threat Growth Analysis

The line graph illustrates the growth of major cybersecurity threats between 2023 and 2025.

#Insights
- DDoS attacks experienced the highest increase over the analyzed period.
- Phishing attacks remained one of the most prevalent threats.
- Malware incidents showed steady growth year over year.
- Ransomware attacks increased significantly, highlighting the need for stronger security controls.

This trend analysis demonstrates the evolving threat landscape and emphasizes the importance of proactive cybersecurity measures.
